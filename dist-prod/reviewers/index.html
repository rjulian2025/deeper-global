Reviewers and contributors

Who is associated with our answers

Some Deeper Global answers name a clinical reviewer, clinical contributor, or editorial reviewer. Labels and dates depend on the attribution type; specialty-based contributor assignments do not imply page-by-page clinical review.

Rick Julian

Editorial Reviewer · Spirituality & Meaning

Content reviewer for spirituality, meaning, and philosophical inquiry

Rick Julian is the author of The Way | A Modern Tao Te Ching, a bestselling reinterpretation of one of the world's most enduring philosophical texts. As a philosopher, speaker, and teacher, he brings decades of study in Taoist and Western philosophical traditions to questions of meaning, purpose, faith transitions, and how people navigate existential uncertainty.

Dr. Alex Crenshaw, PhD

Clinical reviewer · Adult ADHD Testing & Psychological Evaluation

Clinical reviewer for adult ADHD testing and psychological evaluation

Dr. Alex Crenshaw is a licensed psychologist with a PhD in clinical psychology. He conducts adult psychological testing and evidence-based psychotherapy at Peachtree Psychology in Roswell, Georgia, and serves as Assistant Professor of Psychology at Kennesaw State University.

Michelle Morris, LPC, LPCC

Clinical reviewer · Imago Couples Therapy & Neuroscience-Informed Relationships

Clinical reviewer for Imago Relationship Therapy and couples conflict patterns

Michelle Morris, LPC, LPCC is a licensed professional counselor with more than three decades of clinical experience. She is a Certified Imago Relationship Therapist who helps couples understand how nervous-system patterns drive conflict and how structured dialogue can create lasting change.

Kenneth W. Christian, PhD

Clinical contributor · Performance, Purpose & Self-Limiting Patterns

Kenneth W. Christian, PhD is a psychologist and performance psychology specialist whose work focuses on underachievement, self-limiting behavior, procrastination, perfectionism, and the psychological barriers that keep capable people from acting on their potential. He is the author of Your Own Worst Enemy: Breaking the Habit of Adult Underachievement.
