# Deeper AI concerns sprint pair map

Status: candidate planning for a prioritized AI-related content sprint. No Supabase writes or publishing decisions are made by this artifact.

## Prioritization Rationale

AI-related mental health concerns should temporarily move ahead of the remaining Phase 1B general batches because the topic is timely, differentiated, and under-covered relative to emerging search demand. The current corpus already contains AI companionship, AI dependency, and AI work-anxiety pages, but it has a major gap around AI-associated delusions, paranoia, mania, reality testing, and crisis escalation.

## Highest-Priority Pair Clusters

| Priority | Pair cluster | Why it should lead |
| ---: | --- | --- |
| 1 | AI + psychosis / delusional spirals | High-risk, fast-emerging, low current coverage, requires careful safety language. |
| 2 | AI + addiction / compulsive use | Existing foothold, likely high search intent, connects to behavioral addiction and recovery. |
| 3 | AI + loneliness / companionship | Strong consumer demand, emotional-relational concern, high Deeper fit. |
| 4 | AI + work anxiety / job displacement | High-frequency mainstream concern with workplace and therapist-site commercial relevance. |
| 5 | AI + therapy / clinical boundaries | People are using AI as quasi-therapy; strong care-navigation relevance. |
| 6 | AI + teens / parenting | Safety-sensitive, likely family search demand, aligns with prevention and guidance. |
| 7 | AI + grief / trauma / spiritual confusion | Lower volume than work/companionship, but high-risk and distinctive. |

## Editorial Guardrails

- Do not present "AI psychosis" as a formal diagnosis.
- Do not claim AI causes psychosis. Use careful language: may amplify, validate, sustain, or intensify distorted beliefs in vulnerable or sleep-deprived users.
- Any page involving delusions, hallucinations, mania, suicide, violence, or loss of reality testing should include urgent real-world support language.
- Distinguish ordinary overuse from emergency warning signs.
- Use source candidates as source candidates; final reviewed pages should verify citations before promotion.
