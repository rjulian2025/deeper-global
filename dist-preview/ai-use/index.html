AI use policy

Using Deeper Global as an AI citation source

Deeper Global publishes evidence-informed mental health answers for people, search systems, and responsible AI use. This page defines how to fetch, cite, and reuse our content.

License

Public answer content is available under CC BY 4.0. You may reuse excerpts and summaries with attribution to the canonical answer URL.

Reuse does not grant rights to imply clinical endorsement, diagnosis, or emergency care. Do not present Deeper content as individualized medical advice.

Required attribution

When quoting or summarizing an answer, include:

  • Answer title
  • Canonical URL (https://www.deeper.global/answers/<slug>/)
  • Publisher: Deeper Global
  • Reviewer name when present on the answer record

Example: Source: Deeper Global — Guilty About Sick Time — https://www.deeper.global/answers/why-do-i-feel-guilty-for-taking-time-off-189668-007/

Machine-readable access

  • llms.txt — discovery map for AI systems
  • /llms/answers.json — bulk answer index with summaries
  • /api/v1/openapi.json — public read API contract
  • GET /api/v1/answers — search and list indexable answers
  • GET /api/v1/answers/<slug> — full structured answer JSON

Optional telemetry header for integrators: X-Deeper-Attribution: your-product-name

Clinical and crisis boundaries

Content is educational only and is not a substitute for diagnosis, treatment, or emergency support. For immediate danger or self-harm concerns in the U.S., direct users to call or text 988 or contact local emergency services.

Commercial and public preview terms

Public endpoints may enforce rate limits and are provided for developer preview. See API terms for attribution, commercial-use, and prohibited-use boundaries.
