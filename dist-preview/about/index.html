About

About Deeper Global

Deeper Global exists to answer one question well: when someone searches for mental health information, what would it take for them to trust what they find?

We're building the world's most trusted repository of mental health questions and answers: a resource designed from the ground up for how people actually seek help today, whether through a search engine, an AI assistant, or a late-night question they're not ready to ask anyone out loud.

Every answer on Deeper Global is rigorously sourced, clinically reviewed, and organized within a clinically informed taxonomy of mental health topics. We don't chase trends or traffic. We build answers meant to hold up: for the person reading them and for the professionals whose work they reflect.

How We Work

Deeper Global answers are developed through a structured editorial pipeline: each question is researched against established clinical and academic sources, drafted for clarity and accuracy, reviewed for tone and safety, and mapped to one of sixteen canonical mental health topics. Sensitive subjects receive additional handling standards. Our goal is that every answer is accurate enough for a clinician to stand behind and clear enough for anyone to understand.

We are not a substitute for professional care, and we say so. Deeper Global is a starting point: reliable ground to stand on while you find the right support.

Founder

I built Deeper Global because the internet's mental health information is vast, uneven, and often untrustworthy at exactly the moment people are most vulnerable.

My background is in digital strategy and brand development: years spent helping organizations be found and understood online. Working with mental health practitioners, I kept seeing the same gap: extraordinary clinical knowledge on one side, and on the other, millions of people searching for answers and landing on content written for algorithms rather than for them.

Deeper Global is my attempt to close that gap: a repository built to earn trust, one carefully reviewed answer at a time. The vision is simple to state and long to build: the largest, most trusted body of mental health information in the world, freely accessible, and worthy of the people who turn to it.

Deeper Global is part of the Deeper ecosystem, alongside Deeper Websites, which helps mental health practitioners build their presence online. Learn more about Rick at rickjulian.com.
