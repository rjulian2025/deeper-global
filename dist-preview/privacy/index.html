Privacy

Privacy is part of the product.

Deeper Global should be architected to learn from aggregate demand without turning sensitive mental health intent into careless tracking.

Current posture

The v1 site is a public information resource. It does not require user accounts, personal health records, or self-reported symptom profiles.

Deeper uses Google Analytics to understand aggregate site usage, including answer views, topic views, searches, source engagement, crisis-resource clicks, and outbound referral clicks. These events are used for internal product, safety, and editorial planning.

Intent analytics boundary

Search and content events are structured to avoid user accounts, personal health records, full referrer URLs, zip-level reporting, or individual mental health profiles. Search events may include normalized query language and result counts so Deeper can identify coverage gaps, but reporting should stay aggregate-first.

Deeper does not use mental health content behavior for sensitive ad retargeting, does not sell raw query logs, and does not combine educational browsing with personally identifiable health data.

Future monetization boundary

Referrals, sponsored placements, and partner analytics should be designed around aggregate signals, clear disclosures, and strict separation from personally identifiable health data.
