Clinical overview

Assessment and diagnostic expertise

Kelsey Madsen (née Donahue) is a compassionate, empathetic, and open-minded Licensed Professional Counselor (LPC014278) in Georgia with six years of experience. She specializes in working with children , adolescents, and adults dealing with anxiety and depression and eating disorders , as well as children with behavioral disorders like ADHD and ODD. She frequently supports kids navigating childhood anxiety , school stress, and family conflict.

She received her Master's in Clinical Mental Health Counseling from Augusta University and is licensed by the State of Georgia. Her first goal is to build rapport and trust with her clients to ensure progress in the therapeutic journey. She offers both in-person and telehealth therapy.

Kelsey uses a Cognitive-Behavioral approach to help clients change negative thoughts into positive thoughts. She tailors the therapeutic modality to each unique client, utilizing CBT, DBT, Emotionally Focused, Mindfulness-Based (MBCT), Motivational Interviewing, and Strength-Based approaches.
