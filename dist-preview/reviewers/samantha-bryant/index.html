Clinical overview

Assessment and diagnostic expertise

Samantha Bryant is a Master's level Nationally Certified Counselor (NCC) and a Licensed Professional Counselor (LPC) in the state of Georgia (License #LPC013018). She obtained her Master of Science Degree in Clinical Mental Health Counseling from Capella University (Graduated 2019). She has been in practice for 6 years.

Samantha provides mental health counseling in person and telehealth counseling online to residents of Georgia. She has experience working with tweens, adolescents, adults, older adults, and couples. She employs an integrative approach, focusing on helping clients work towards their own goals and building on personal strengths.

Her therapeutic approach is tailored to fit each individual's needs, utilizing modalities including Acceptance and Commitment (ACT), Cognitive Behavioral (CBT), Gottman Method, Humanistic, Integrative, Multicultural, Person-Centered, Positive Psychology, and Strength-Based approaches. She offers evening and weekend appointments.
