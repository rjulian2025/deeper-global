Clinical overview

Assessment and diagnostic expertise

Lauren Sanders is a Licensed Professional Counselor (LPC009048) with a Master of Science in Mental Health Counseling from Georgia State University (2013) and an additional Master's degree in Child Development from the University of Alabama (2002). She is a Nationally Certified Counselor (NCC) with over 14 years of experience and has been in practice for 12 years.

Lauren is a Certified Clinical Anxiety Treatment Professional (CCATP), intensively trained in OCD/pediatric OCD through the International OCD Foundation's Behavior Therapy Training Institute. Her practice focuses on helping clients with anxiety , body dysmorphia, chronic illness , emetophobia, grief , OCD , parenting, panic disorder, perfectionism, phobias, and other life stressors. Her pediatric training makes her an excellent fit for childhood anxiety and OCD .

She has a unique skill set working with children and adults, helping them and their loved ones cope with chronic illness and grief. She uses a compassionate approach utilizing Cognitive Behavioral Therapy, Exposure Response Prevention, and Solution Focused Counseling to help clients reach their full potential by gaining perspective, embracing healthy change, and learning to cope with life's anxieties.
