Clinical overview

Assessment and diagnostic expertise

Lexi Cooper earned her Bachelor of Science in Communication Sciences and Disorders from the University of Georgia. She then completed her Master of Social Work (LMSW) at UGA, along with a Certificate in Marriage and Family Therapy. During her time at UGA, Lexi provided mental health therapy to the UGA athletes through the UGA Mental Health and Performance Clinic. She also earned a Master of Science in Speech-Language Pathology (M.S., CCC-SLP) from Nova Southeastern University.

Lexi has worked with clients across the lifespan but specializes in children , adolescents, and young adults. She supports individuals experiencing anxiety , depression, ADHD , and OCD , and provides tools for those coping with academic and sports performance concerns.
