Editorial policy

How Deeper Global publishes mental health answers

Deeper Global is an educational mental health answer engine. This policy describes how content is created, reviewed, and updated without overclaiming clinical authority.

Educational purpose

Answers are written to help people understand common mental health questions in clear language. They are not a substitute for diagnosis, treatment, or emergency care.

AI-assisted drafting with human accountability

Deeper may use AI tools to help structure and draft answers. Publication still requires human editorial accountability, categorization, citation discipline, and risk awareness aligned with our Trust Protocol.

When a named reviewer is listed on an answer, that reflects the reviewer recorded for that page. When no reviewer is listed, the answer should not be interpreted as individually clinician-reviewed.

Sources and evidence posture

The Protocol calls for a source set on each answer. Sources appear on answer pages only when they have been added to that answer’s record. Deeper does not invent citations or imply clinical review that has not occurred.

Risk-sensitive content

Answers involving suicide, self-harm, crisis, abuse, medication, or similar topics receive stricter handling. Crisis-sensitive pages include visible support guidance, including U.S. 988 where appropriate.

Updates and corrections

Answers include an updated date when content changes. Batch-generated legacy content is being upgraded to Deeper’s current review standards over time.
